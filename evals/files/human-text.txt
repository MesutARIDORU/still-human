My dad kept every receipt from 1987 to 2003 in shoeboxes labelled by year, and when we cleared out the garage last spring I found out he'd also kept the shoeboxes' receipts. I'm not joking. There's a slip from a Clarks in Leeds for the very box it was stapled inside.

I still don't know what to make of it. Mum says he wasn't a hoarder, he was "thorough", which is the kind of word she uses when she's decided not to argue. Half the paper had faded to nothing anyway — thermal ink doesn't last — so we sat on the floor holding blank strips up to the light like idiots, trying to read the ghost of a petrol price.

We kept 1994. No reason. It was the year I broke my arm, but there's nothing about that in there, just a lot of Woolworths and something from a garden centre that cost £4.99.
