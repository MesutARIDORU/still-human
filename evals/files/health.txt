Taking your new medication

We understand that starting a new medication can feel overwhelming, and it's completely normal to have questions. You're not alone on this journey.

Metformin plays a crucial role in helping to manage blood sugar levels in people with type 2 diabetes. Take one 500 mg tablet twice a day with meals. Your doctor may potentially increase your dose after two weeks.

Some people may experience side effects such as nausea, diarrhoea or stomach pain. These effects could possibly improve over time as your body adjusts. If you get severe stomach pain, vomiting or unusual tiredness, stop taking the tablets and contact your doctor straight away.

It is important to note that you should not drink alcohol in large amounts while taking this medicine. Remember, your health is your most valuable asset!
